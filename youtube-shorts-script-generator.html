Why a structured Shorts script matters

YouTube Shorts live or die in the first three seconds. A weak hook tanks retention before the algorithm has a chance to push the video. A strong hook plus a clear 0-60 second structure does the opposite. This generator is built around that single fact: you ship a tighter script, you keep more viewers, and YouTube rewards the result.

What every generated script includes

  • Hook (0-3s) - a contradiction, a promise, or a curiosity gap.
  • Beat map (4-55s) - on-screen text, B-roll cues, and pacing notes.
  • Payoff and second loop (36-55s) - the moment that earns a comment.
  • Engagement prompt and CTA (56-60s) - one easy question, no friction.
  • Safety check - filtered against YouTube's Community Guidelines and advertiser-friendly rules.

How to use it well

  1. Pick a topic with a clear curiosity gap or transformation promise.
  2. Choose a content lane (education, entertainment, or hybrid).
  3. Generate three scripts. Pick the angle that feels most like you.
  4. Rewrite the hook in your own voice - it is the only line that has to feel native.
  5. Record and ship. Compare retention curves between angles next time.
