Education lane

Education YouTube Shorts script generator

Education Shorts have a different problem from entertainment Shorts: viewers will swipe past anything that even smells like a lecture. The fix is a 60-second structure that opens with a hook, pays off a single idea, and ends on a question the viewer wants to answer in the comments. That is what this generator writes for you.

→ Open the generator and pick the Education content lane.

What an education Short looks like in 60 seconds

  • 0-3s hook - a counterintuitive fact, a misconception, or a transformation promise.
  • 4-25s setup - the one concept the viewer needs to understand the payoff.
  • 26-45s payoff - the single, memorable idea. One concept per Short.
  • 46-55s example - one concrete case viewers will recognize.
  • 56-60s CTA - a question that surfaces a personal example in the comments.

Education topic prompts that work

  • "The biology reason you forget names two seconds after meeting someone."
  • "3 history facts your textbook quietly skipped."
  • "Why this math shortcut feels illegal."
  • "The one sentence that changes how you read every news headline."
  • "3 study tricks backed by research that sound fake."

Avoid these education-Short mistakes

  • Two ideas per Short. Cut to one. The other is its own video.
  • "Today I'm going to tell you about..." Open with the payoff promise instead.
  • Reading a Wikipedia paragraph on camera. Convert facts into a contradiction or a curiosity gap.
  • No CTA. Education viewers love sharing their own examples - ask for one.
