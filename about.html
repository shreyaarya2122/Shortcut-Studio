About

ShortCut Studio helps creators turn ideas into short-form scripts.

ShortCut Studio is a free tool for YouTube Shorts creators who need faster hooks, clearer 60-second structures, and simple engagement prompts. The generator is designed for education and entertainment topics, with output focused on retention, visual pacing, and audience interaction.

Who it is for

The tool is useful for solo creators, students, educators, entertainment channels, and small teams that want a repeatable structure before recording or editing a Short.

How it works

Users enter a topic, select a content lane, audience, tone, and pacing style, then receive three script angles. Each result includes a hook, beat map, on-screen text suggestions, engagement prompt, and style tips.
